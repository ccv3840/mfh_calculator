Loan Evaluation

What this tool models

Compares 4 wealth-building strategies using configurable initial capital + annual salary over up to 25 years. All results recalculate live with every slider or input change.

New features: flexible loan repayment modes (salary / interest-only / custom years), private initial capital loan, MFH cost breakdown charts, LTV trajectory, second MFH purchase modelling, and direct value input on all fields.

All MFH uses correct German Annuitätendarlehen math with AfA depreciation shield and 42% marginal tax rate. Not financial advice.

4 strategies compared

  • Option 1 (A) — Swiss Lombard + Small MFH: Borrow at low rate against ETF collateral at Swiss bank. Flexible repayment: pay down from salary, keep as cheap debt (interest-only), or custom schedule.
  • Option 2 (B) — Grundschuld + Small MFH: Refinance existing property (Grundschuld). Fixed period or interest-only, repay at exit.
  • Option 3 (C) — All-in Large MFH: All capital as MFH closing costs. No acquisition loan. Salary fully compounds in S&P500.
  • Option 4 (D) — Pure S&P500: Lump sum + DCA salary into index. Zero debt, zero operations. Benchmark.

Key inputs & assumptions

  • Click any value badge to type directly
  • MFH closing costs (Nebenkosten) ≈ 10% of purchase price
  • AfA: 2% × 70% of purchase price per year
  • CGT on exit: 0% after 10yr Spekulationsfrist (personal)
  • Interest-only Lombard: all salary → S&P500 from yr 1; principal repaid from ETF at exit
  • Private loan: optional cost of initial capital (hidden from bank)
  • Second MFH: uses MFH1 equity after X years as down payment
